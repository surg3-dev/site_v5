# 7. THE MOST IMPORTANT INTERACTION DESIGN REQUIREMENT

The website must be a **VERTICAL SCROLLING EXPERIENCE WITH SELECTIVE HORIZONTAL SCROLL SECTIONS.**

Do NOT make the entire website horizontally scroll.

Instead:

Normal vertical scrolling

↓

A section becomes pinned

↓

Vertical mouse-wheel/touchpad input drives horizontal movement

↓

The content travels horizontally

↓

The horizontal sequence completes

↓

The section releases

↓

Normal vertical scrolling resumes

↓

Another section can later use the same interaction

This is commonly described as:

**pinned scroll-driven horizontal storytelling**

or

**scroll-driven horizontal sections.**

Implement this elegantly.

The user should never feel trapped.

The transition between vertical and horizontal movement should feel natural and intentional.

Do not abruptly hijack the scroll.

Use smooth interpolation and excellent easing.
